# 🏦 Branch Performance and Data-Driven Growth Strategy for Darcy & Bennet Bank (2020–2022)

This project represents an applied data analytics case study I carried out on Darcy & Bennet Bank, focusing on its three major UK branches in Manchester, Liverpool, and London.  
The goal was to evaluate branch performance, identify growth opportunities, and demonstrate how analytics can guide evidence-based business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Aim

The objective of this study was to apply the CRISP-DM analytical framework to:

- Evaluate the operational and financial performance of the three branches  
- Identify the best-performing city for future branch expansion  
- Measure the impact of the renovation carried out at the Manchester branch  
- Provide practical recommendations for data-driven decision-making in the banking sector  

---

## ⚠️ Identified Problem

At the start of the project, the dataset presented several challenges such as missing values, inconsistent date formats, and outliers across branches.  
These data quality issues made it difficult to draw accurate insights and highlighted the need for improved data governance within the bank.  

To ensure reliability, I began by cleaning, validating, and standardising the dataset before conducting the analysis.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

### 1. Manchester Branch Performance
Manchester consistently outperformed Liverpool and London across all metrics.  
After renovation, the branch achieved the highest customer volume of 27,571 and a total deposit value of £89.1 million, confirming that structural and service upgrades had a strong positive impact.

### 2. Steady Growth Over Time
From 2020 to 2022, both customer volume and deposit values showed steady growth.  
The average deposit per customer rose from £276 in 2020 to £323 in 2022, reflecting improved customer trust and product engagement.

### 3. Seasonal Activity Patterns
Activity peaked during July to September each year, reflecting promotional campaigns and broader economic cycles influencing savings behaviour.

### 4. Product Category Insights
Traditional savings accounts performed best across all branches, followed by money market and ISA accounts.  
Cash management accounts recorded the weakest performance, indicating limited customer interest in complex savings products.

### 5. Data Quality and Integrity Improvements
I addressed missing data using imputation and removed incomplete records where necessary.  
Schema mapping and field validation were implemented to standardise branch-level datasets, ensuring a reliable data source for all analyses.

## 💡 Practical Recommendations

- Expand branch operations in Manchester due to its high customer base and deposit growth  
- Renovate and modernise the Liverpool branch using the Manchester model to boost customer satisfaction  
- Develop internal analytics dashboards to monitor deposit growth, customer acquisition, and seasonal patterns  
- Redesign the marketing strategy for the London branch with competitive rates and targeted digital campaigns  
- Focus on promoting traditional and money market savings products to sustain long-term profitability  
- Establish continuous data monitoring systems to maintain data quality and support decision-making  

---

## 🧠 Reflections

This project demonstrated how data analytics can drive operational and strategic transformation within financial institutions.  
By applying the CRISP-DM model and focusing on practical data issues, I was able to turn raw transaction data into clear, actionable insights.  
The results confirmed that renovation, improved customer experience, and evidence-based management collectively enhance performance in retail banking.

---

## 🧩 Tools and Methods

- Framework: CRISP-DM (Cross-Industry Standard Process for Data Mining)  
- Techniques: Data cleaning, imputation, trend analysis, and visualisation  
- KPIs: Deposit value per customer, client volume growth, product performance, branch profitability, and customer satisfaction  
- Tools used: Excel, Python (Pandas, Matplotlib), and Power BI  

---

## ✅ Conclusion

The findings indicate that Manchester is the most viable location for future expansion.  
By continuing to integrate analytics into strategic planning, Darcy & Bennet Bank can strengthen its market position, improve customer experience, and achieve sustainable growth across all branches.
